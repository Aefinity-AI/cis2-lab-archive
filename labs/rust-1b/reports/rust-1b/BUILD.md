## Budget arithmetic for box1 (S4). ESTIMATES from the bar's expected speeds, not timings; nothing was timed.
Inputs: registered cap 28,800 s; used 6,735 s (read from ~/.rust1_budget_box1 over ssh, one line "6735 2026-10-04T11:18:43Z OK"); left 22,065 s (6.13 h). The operator's estimates from the bar's expected speeds: one job that holds phase n1 and phase n2 with 5 reps takes about 4.1 h = 14,760 s; the 10-rep redo of phase n1 about 3.7 h = 13,320 s.
Model (my reading of how those two figures are built, stated so it can be checked): a "round" is one process of every arm of a phase, which is one rep or one check set (before or after the reps); a phase n1 job has 5 reps + 2 check sets = 7 rounds (the redo 10 + 2 = 12 rounds), a phase n2 job 7 rounds; per job a fixed part F for hash check, venv check, idle baseline of 6 x 20 s and the first idle gate, assumed F = 360 s. Redo: F + 12 c1 = 13,320 gives c1 = 1,080 s per n1 round (18 min, idle waits included). N=1 job: 360 + 7 x 1,080 = 7,920 s (2.2 h). The single 5-rep job of both phases: F + 7 c1 + 7 c2 = 14,760 gives 7 c2 = 6,840 s, c2 = 977 s per n2 round (0.90 of an n1 round: 7 arms, no X, at 2 threads). N=2 job: 360 + 6,840 = 7,200 s (2.0 h).
| plan on box1 | box-seconds | against 22,065 s left |
|---|---|---|
| as it was: one job (both phases), then the 10-rep redo | 14,760 + 13,320 = 28,080 | does not fit, 6,015 s short (the finding of the last round) |
| N=1 job, then N=2 job (no redo) | 7,920 + 7,200 = 15,120 | fits, 6,945 s left |
| N=1 job, then the redo, then the N=2 job | 7,920 + 13,320 = 21,240 for the first two | fits with 825 s left (3.7 percent: a few voids, each about one arm run, can cap the last arm of the redo); the N=2 job then finds 825 s < 7,200 s and ends COST-CAPPED, nothing timed |
| N=1 job, then the N=2 job, then a redo | 15,120, then 13,320 wanted with 6,945 left | the redo does not fit: this is the order the ruling forbids |
So the fixed order is: the N=1 job before any N=2 work; the redo (if a label is INCONCLUSIVE) before the N=2 job; the N=2 job runs only if the budget left covers 7,200 s (its wrapper tests it), otherwise N=2 on box1 is reported COST-CAPPED. This narrows what can be claimed and makes nothing easier; phase n1 on box1 carries every label. Margins are thin: the model is two figures and one assumed F; every void costs one arm run (a few minutes) of the total; the runner's own cap test stops an arm that would not fit and records it COST-CAPPED. A rust-1 redo (rust1redo-box1, 3.7 to 10.9 h by its own estimate) draws on the same 22,065 s and is not counted here: the operator sets the priority between the two redos.
box2 and box3 (unlabelled; about 3 times box1 by the bar): a single job of both phases about 44,280 s (12.3 h) on box2, phase n1 alone about 23,760 s on box3, against a cap of 86,400 s each minus what rust-1 used (box2 job rust1-box2 ended INVALID after 2,514 s).
